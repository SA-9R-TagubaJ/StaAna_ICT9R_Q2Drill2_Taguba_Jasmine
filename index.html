Home Page 🦋

Founded in 1966 by Dr. Preciosa S. Soliven, the O.B. Montessori Center (OBMC) is a progressive, co-educational pre-school to senior high school institution in the Philippines that implements Dr. Maria Montessori's holistic educational framework. Blending traditional academic rigor with child-centered independence, responsibility, and experiential learning, the school operates across multiple campuses—including Greenhills, Fairview, Las Piñas, Sta. Ana (Manila), and Angeles (Pampanga)—guiding students from early Casa programs through primary, intermediate, and secondary levels.
